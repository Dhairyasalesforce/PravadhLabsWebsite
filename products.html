PravadhIQ

Ship it. Prove it. PravadhIQ is GitHub-native Salesforce DevOps and data migration on one platform: AI risk analysis on every release, generated rollback plans, data moves with AI field mapping, and a SHA-256 audit chain behind every change.

The problem

Releases, migrations and the audit should not be three jobs.

Most teams still run releases, data movement and the audit with three tools, three owners and three logins. When something breaks, nobody can say quickly what changed, who approved it, or how to roll it back.

PravadhIQ puts them on one platform. Releases run from GitHub with a risk score and a rollback plan. Data moves with AI field mapping and a check before anything is written. Every action lands on the same tamper-evident chain.

GitHub-native CI/CD into the org you already run. Every change sits on a branch, gets reviewed, and is risk-scored before it lands.

  • GitHub today; Bitbucket and Azure DevOps on the roadmap
MOVE

Data migration

Move records into Salesforce with AI field mapping from EMIE, transformations you can preview, and a check before anything is written.

  • Reusable, versioned transformation chains

A SHA-256 chain of custody from source commit to destination record: who did it, what it touched, and why the risk was scored that way.

The problem

A list is not a plan. A sequencer is not a next send.

Contact databases dump names. Sequencers dump cadences. The CRM stores what you already know. None of them tell a rep who is actually ready — or protect the mailbox while they find out. Signals sit in the pipeline, the inbox, the website, and a sheet nobody opens twice.

Pravadh SI discovers, ranks, and drafts — then waits. New mailboxes ramp. Duplicates are skipped. A bounce guard pauses the sender. A reply stops the cadence. Anything that would reach a real person still waits for your approval.

  • We never scrape LinkedIn

Pravadh DocSign

Any object. Any document. Any signature. Pravadh DocSign is document generation and eSignature that lives inside Salesforce. Generate. Sign. Store. Automate. The signed file lands in Salesforce Files on the record.

Free and open source under the Apache 2.0 licence — the full source is on GitHub. Unlimited users, documents, recipients, and signatures. No licence to sign, and no paid tier. See how it sits against Conga, S-Docs, and Nintex.

Visit pravadhdocsign.pravadhlabs.com
The problem

One free app instead of three paid tools.

Teams still buy a document generator, a signature tool, and a place to store the file — then stitch them back onto the Salesforce record. Recipients need a licence. Caps show up after the pilot. S-Docs, Conga, and Nintex are paid.

Pravadh DocSign does the whole path inside the org. Any standard or custom object. Templates in PDF and Word. Upload and prepare. Built-in eSign. A SHA-256 chain, re-checked on every view. Flow actions to generate, send, check status, cancel, and resend. Agentforce when it is available.
