Questions teams ask first.

Something else? Ask us directly.

Do you charge for AI tokens?

No. EMIE, our built-in AI agent, is included at no extra cost. If you connect your own AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT through MCP, your AI provider bills you for that usage under your own plan. We never add a token or credit charge.

What is the Early Access Program?

Selected teams get six months of free access to the Pravadh products they're approved for, priority onboarding and regular sessions with our team. Applications are reviewed individually, based on product fit and availability. Apply here.

Does the AI act without asking?

No. Agents prepare the work, then wait. Deploys, emails, merges and shortlists only happen after a person approves them, and every approval is recorded.

Where does our CRM data live?

CRM records stay in your org. Pravadh products work with the systems you already run instead of copying your data somewhere new.

Do we need Salesforce?

PravadhIQ and DocSign are built for Salesforce. Pravadh SI, Caliber, Pravadh PM and GovernX work for business teams on other CRMs and systems too.

Is DocSign really free?

Yes. DocSign is free and open source under Apache 2.0, with unlimited users, documents and signatures. It isn't part of the Early Access Program because there's nothing to pay.
