The problem 02 / 15

Enterprise teams run change as three disconnected jobs.

Deployment, migration, and governance each get their own tool, owner, and blind spot. A deploy can succeed while the release still breaks downstream.

  • DevOps — Copado, Gearset, AutoRABIT, Change Sets
  • Data — Informatica, MuleSoft, Data Loader, one-off scripts
  • Governance — spreadsheet audits, tribal knowledge, log digging
  • Delivery — Jira chrome, feature-gated PM, no white-label or on-prem
Pain 01

No single source of truth

Who changed what, what moved, and who approved it lives in three systems — if it was recorded at all.

Pain 02

Field mapping intelligence lives in people, not systems. Every new migration starts from zero.

Pain 03

Consultancies can't own the stack

Client-facing teams need Jira-grade delivery under their brand — or on their servers — without a source license.
