PravadhIQ

Deploy code. Ready the data. Govern everything. PravadhIQ starts from an industry cloud, brings records from the systems that hold them, and places each fact where that industry says it belongs — one record per customer, with a person on every write.

Foundation first. Salesforce is one destination, not the product. See how the rail is put together.

Visit pravadhiq.pravadhlabs.com
The problem

A clean deploy is not a ready customer.

Most teams still run releases, data movement, and the audit as three jobs — three owners, three logins, and no single record of the customer. A package can succeed and still leave the same person split across CRM, billing, orders, and a ticket queue.

PravadhIQ treats Foundation as the control room. An industry cloud decides what each system may hold. Any listed source to any listed destination. People approve writes. Releases and the records behind them share the same evidence.

Modules

Foundation first. Then ship and govern.

FOUNDATION

One record per customer

Start from an industry cloud, then the systems you bring data from and the systems where it should be ready. Salesforce is one destination — alongside the rest of the stack you already run.

  • Industry cloud decides what each system may hold
  • Any listed source to any listed destination
  • A person approves before anything is written
  • Borderline matches never merge without a person
SHIP

Governed releases

Pipelines into the org you already run. Every change sits on a branch, gets reviewed, and is risk-scored before it lands.

  • Risk analysis on every diff
  • Generated rollback plans
  • Approvals and an audit trail
  • Environment-to-environment promotion
GOVERN

Prove every action

A chain of custody from source commit to destination record — who did it, what it touched, and why the risk was scored that way.

  • Shared audit chain for code and data
  • Disagreements between systems are shown, not hidden
  • Every value traceable to its source system
  • Release + readiness on one timeline
Who it's for

Built for teams whose customer lives in more than one system.

PravadhIQ is built for consultancies, in-house architects, and RevOps teams who need one record per customer before they ship another release — and who are done running Foundation, pipelines, and the audit as three separate jobs.

Pravadh SI discovers, ranks, and drafts — then waits. New mailboxes ramp. Duplicates are skipped. A bounce guard pauses the sender. A reply stops the cadence. Anything that would reach a real person still waits for your approval.

Pravadh DocSign

Any object. Any document. Any signature. Pravadh DocSign is document generation and eSignature that lives inside Salesforce. Generate. Sign. Store. Automate. The signed file lands in Salesforce Files on the record.

Free and open source under the Apache 2.0 licence — the full source is on GitHub. Unlimited users, documents, recipients, and signatures. No licence to sign, and no paid tier. See how it sits against Conga, S-Docs, and Nintex.

Visit pravadhdocsign.pravadhlabs.com
The problem

One free app instead of three paid tools.

Teams still buy a document generator, a signature tool, and a place to store the file — then stitch them back onto the Salesforce record. Recipients need a licence. Caps show up after the pilot. S-Docs, Conga, and Nintex are paid.

Pravadh DocSign does the whole path inside the org. Any standard or custom object. Templates in PDF and Word. Upload and prepare. Built-in eSign. A SHA-256 chain, re-checked on every view. Flow actions to generate, send, check status, cancel, and resend. Agentforce when it is available.

Modules

Generate. Sign. Store. Automate.

GENERATE

Any object, any document

Templates, PDF, and Word — or upload and prepare a file already on the record.

  • Any standard or custom object
  • Templates, PDF, and Word
  • Upload and prepare
  • Home tab for every document and envelope
SIGN

No licence to sign

Unlimited recipients and signatures. The person signing does not need a Salesforce licence.

  • Built-in eSign
  • Unlimited recipients and signatures
  • Expiry and reminders
  • Branding and consent on the signing site
STORE

Back on the record

The completed file is saved to Salesforce Files on the record, with a chain you can re-check.

  • Salesforce Files on the record
  • SHA-256 audit chain
  • Re-checked on every view
  • Status stays in the org
AUTOMATE

Flow. Then Agentforce.

Generate, send, and track from Flow. A platform event fires on every envelope change.

  • Generate Document and Send for Signature
  • Get Signature Status, Cancel, and Resend
  • Platform event on every envelope change
  • Agentforce when it is available
Who it's for

Built for Salesforce teams who are done paying per signature.

Pravadh DocSign is for admins and architects replacing Conga, S-Docs, or Nintex — and for any org that needs documents and signatures without a per-seat bill. Install the managed package, or deploy the open-source code straight from GitHub. Unlimited users. The product is free and open source.
